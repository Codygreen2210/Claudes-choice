# Inventor D, round 17: public sourced tracker candidates

1. **What it is:** A free dated list of big-tech keynote promises (Apple, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta) with the keynote video link, the promised date, and the first dated release note that shipped each one, so anyone can see announced versus delivered.
2. **Already exists?** Partly. Tesla/Musk slice: tesorb.com/musk-timeline-promises-vs-reality-tracker, hudson-labs.com/research/elon-musk-predictions-tsla. Apple Siri: single-article timeline at scriptbyai.com/apple-intelligence-timeline. No cross-company structured record found. Closest same-shape: regardsofwallstreet.com AI Compute Deal Ledger (free, CC BY 4.0).
3. **Who uses it:** journalists writing delay stories (Daring Fireball, 9to5Mac, The Register), analysts, buyers. Post place: Hacker News, r/apple. Not verified.
4. **Proof number:** one outside article cites it within 60 days. Test: 150 rows for 5 firms, about 10 days of work.
5. **Odds (X in 6):** two weeks 5 / 100 free users in 30 days 1.5 / 10,000 in 6 months 0.3 / bigger builds it in six months 1.

**Searches for an existing version:** about 19 searches and fetches. Wordings: rug pull tracker, AI data centre tracker, keynote promises tracker, ban list, compute deals tracker, enterprise rollout tracker, Apple feature timeline, Musk predictions tracker.

**Other candidates, all dropped:**
- Data centres: Sightline Climate (commercial), Data Center Watch (free report), Epoch AI hub (93 sites, free CC BY, satellite and permits). HN 7 to 14 points; a post disputing the "half cancelled" claim shows the accuracy risk.
- Compute deals: ledger above covers announcements; Epoch covers built capacity. Only the join is missing.
- Rug pulls: Relicensing Monitor, pagecrawl, CHAOSS study. HN 3 to 7 points.
- Enterprise rollouts: surveys only, firms mostly unnamed, defamation risk.
- AI products pulled after errors: not searched, AI Incident Database likely covers it (not opened).
- Tool bans: only news. Not pursued, HN not checked.

**First-user answer:** a visitor types "Siri" and sees each promise, its date, and the delivery date. Works with zero crowd data.
**The data:** one record is promise text, keynote link and timestamp, promised window, delivery date, release-note link. 10,000 records over a decade are a dated promise-keeping history nobody holds in one table. No paying buyer found.
**Finding events:** one person with AI help reads keynote transcripts and release notes. Dates are easy to check, which keeps disputes rare. Rule: only dated, sourced facts; no words like "broken"; show the quoted promise and the release note side by side; corrections by email with a source link, fixed within days and logged publicly.
**Press:** HN for Siri-delay stories got 1 to 12 points (top 12). Stargate stories got 1,021 and 424 points, but that subject is covered above. A household-name hook exists (Apple, Tesla) but the Tesla one is taken.
**How it grows by itself:** weak. Only when a delay story breaks and a writer links it.
**Campaign:** Yes. Hook: "Every Apple Intelligence promise from WWDC 2024 to today, with dates."
**Verdict:** best open one, but weak and low odds. Over budget by about one search.
