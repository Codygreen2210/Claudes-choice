# Report C

Bar chosen: PAYING.

1. What it is: a web page where a small RIA's compliance officer uploads staff brokerage statements (PDF/CSV) each quarter and gets a review sheet against the firm's restricted list, ready for the Rule 204A-1 file.
2. Exists? Yes: Comply/RIA in a Box, Orion Compliance, SmartRIA, ComplianceAlpha, RIA Compliance Technology's Simple Trade Monitor (links in data-C.jsonl).
3. Buyers: SEC-registered RIAs, 16,544 at year-end 2025, median 8 employees (InvestmentNews, opened). At $250 a month, $1M a year needs 333 firms, about 2% of them.
4. Proof number: 3 of 10 small-RIA compliance officers who say they would pay $250 a month after a free test.
5. Odds: two-week version 4 in 6 / 100 free users in 30 days 1 in 6 / 100 paying in 90 days 1 in 20 / $1M in three years 1 in 25 / bigger builds it in six months: already built.

Searches for an existing version: marketing rule tools, TRID tolerance, personal trading review; all exist (Casemark, Seiright, Calyx, Comply, Orion, Simple Trade Monitor).
First-user answer: would work, but the first user must trust a stranger with employee statements (personal data).
The data: no data play; paying only. Comparable prices $3,000 a year (Comply), $8,500 a year (Orion).
How it grows: no evidence; no audience route found.
Campaign: no.
Demand: ZERO dated 2025 or 2026 asks found from real people after 17 lookups; only vendor blogs. Demand unproven.
Regulator: SEC, Advisers Act Rule 204A-1 (opened). Mortgage angle (CFPB TRID) dropped: tools exist.
