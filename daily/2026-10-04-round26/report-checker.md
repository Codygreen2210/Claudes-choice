[memo](https://lslbc.gov/wp-content/uploads/LSLBC_Memorandum_Statutory_Changes_July_13_2026_REVISED.pdf), [6-25 memo](https://lslbc.gov/wp-content/uploads/Memorandum-6-25-2026.pdf), [Kean Miller](https://www.keanmiller.com/insights/blog/louisiana-law-blog/the-2026-lslbc-overhaul-licensing-insurance-enforcement-and-contractor-risk/)

Five real names seen on a mirror, no contact details: Freetown Builders, Air Condition Sales And Service, Doucet Services, Adapt Concepts, Drymax Systems ([statecreds](https://www.statecreds.com/license/la/mold_remediator/city/lafayette)).
