RowProof

Bank statement PDFs to Excel, with every row checked.

Other converters hand you a spreadsheet and hope. RowProof checks each row against the bank's own running balance and the statement's opening and closing totals, then marks what it proved and what you should look at.

Or try a sample (fictional banks)

Reads the PDF itself

Finds every word and number with its exact position on the page, then rebuilds the rows and columns, including descriptions that wrap onto a second line.

Tries each reading

Is that column money out or money in? Signed or not? It tries each way and keeps the one the bank's printed balances agree with.

Proves or flags

Opening balance plus every row has to land on the closing balance. Any row that doesn't fit is marked red, with its page number.

RowProof works on statements that have real text in them. Scanned or photographed statements need OCR first, and it will tell you when that's the case. It's a daily build by Cody Green, made with Claude.
