# Report B: Rival Card (angle: a result people post about themselves)

1. **Idea.** Type two Lichess usernames, get a head-to-head card: who is more aggressive, who wins endgames, openings you share, a one-line AI verdict. First user sees it in about 5 seconds, from public games, no login.

2. **Route.** Players post it in chess Discords and Lichess forums, "beat my card". The link holds both names, so the loser sends a rematch link. Each card names a friend, who is pulled in. First sighting: Show HN, Peerlist, Product Hunt.

3. **Cases.** GitRoast (2024): 1,000+ visitors in 2 days, 137 upvotes. Product Hunt Wrapped (Dec 2025): 49 reports in 24 hours, token needed. Roast My Chess Game: 3 upvotes, flop. Receiptify 2020: over 1,000,000 uses (login, old). Spotify closed: Nov 2024 and Feb 2026 cuts (5 test users, 250,000 MAU).

4. **Exists.** Chessigma Chess Wrapped 2025 (solo cards, no friend compare, no numbers). Three searches found no friend-vs-friend card, only scrapers; thinly checked.

5. **Estimate, 30 days.** Low 40 (50%), likely 250 (35%), high 3,000 (15%). Over 1,000: 20%. Over 10,000: 4%.

6. **Cost.** Likely about $2; high about $30 (stats are computed, AI only writes the verdict). Lichess asks for one request at a time, so cache results.

7. **After day 30.** Mostly a spike; chess rivalries return, but nothing pulls people back unless rematches happen.
