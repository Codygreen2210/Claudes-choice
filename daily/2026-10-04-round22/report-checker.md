## A: Data Act Switch Page. Verdict: WEAKENED, close to killed
- **Real duty:** Latham says the switching rules have applied since 12 Sep 2025, all switching charges are banned from 12 Jan 2027, and small providers get no carve-out (only custom-built or test services). https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-data-act-significant-new-switching-requirements-due-to-take-effect-for-data-processing-services

Law firms such as SKW Schwarz sell a fixed-fee addendum (https://www.skwschwarz.de/en/Cloud-Switching-Addendum). The Commission has non-binding model clauses. No one-click generator found in two wordings, nothing on Product Hunt or GitHub.

- **Spread:** none. Buyers are founders who do not talk to each other, and law firms already own the search results.
- **Fix:** none cheap; only a one-off paid document test.
