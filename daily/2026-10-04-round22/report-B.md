# Report B (round 22)

**Bar chosen: PAYING.**

1. **What it is:** "Vendor Trust Link": a small software company pastes its domain; the page drafts its subprocessor list (from the public scripts and mail records of its site), a standard DPA and a single link to send to any customer who asks. The customer opens a page showing the list, a sign button and a "tell me when this changes" box, all with a small "made with" footer. Spread is built into the output.
2. **Exists?** Partly. Visualping gives a free alert button for the subscribing side (https://visualping.io/subprocessor). Free DPA text generators exist (https://insertchat.com/free-tools/data-processing-agreement-generator). Vanta and others bundle trust centers but list no price (https://getpulsesignal.com/pricing/vanta). I found no one-click hosted publisher, but my search was thin.
3. **Who pays:** small software companies with a first EU or enterprise customer. Ask found: Nov 16, 2025 (https://yomotherboard.com/question/do-we-need-a-data-processing-agreement-for-our-first-european-client/).
4. **Number that proves it:** how many of 20 founders asked for a DPA say they would pay $15 a month for the link. Cheapest test: reply to open threads by hand.
5. **Odds:** working version in two weeks 4 in 6 / 100 free users in 30 days 2 in 6 / 100 paying in 90 days 1 in 20 / $1M a year within three years 2% / someone bigger builds it in six months 2 in 6.

**Searches for an existing version:** "subprocessor list page generator", "subprocessor list hosted page subscribe", "free DPA generator signable link", "subprocessor page builder one-click". Found alert monitors and text generators, no publisher.
**First-user answer:** a draft list and DPA for their own domain with zero crowd data. Weak spot: scanning a site only shows front-end vendors, not back-end ones like email or payment tools, so the draft is incomplete.
**The data:** none sold. Not a data business.
**Arithmetic:** price anchors are EUR 4.99 to 19.99 a month (iubenda) and $10 a month (Termly). At $15 a month, $1M a year needs about 5,600 paying customers. No evidence of that many.
**How it grows:** each customer who receives the link sees the footer. Named example: Tally, Sept 2020 start, no audience, "Powered by Tally" badge one of four main channels, 11k users and $5k MRR by Oct 2021 (https://blog.tally.so/year-1-how-we-bootstrapped-tally-to-11k-users-and-5k-mrr/). Honest limit: Product Hunt and Twitter also drove it. The recipients here are legal and procurement staff, who rarely build software or buy for themselves.
**Campaign:** no.

**Proof count (data-B.jsonl):** 5 price records, 1 qualifying ask (the Indie Hackers one is from 2019 and does not count). Minimum of 3 asks not met. Demand is unproven.
**Legal risk:** a generated DPA is legal paperwork; wording must say it is a template, not advice.
**Verdict:** weak. Close to the already-discussed subprocessor ground, thin asks, low price.
