# Report D: web game portals (CrazyGames first)

1. **Idea.** One small browser game submitted to CrazyGames, free to players, paid by ad share. I did not verify a thinly supplied genre, so no specific game is named. No customer price.

2. **Base rate.** Failed. I found zero dated makers with $1,000 a month from portal ads. Found: DonislawDev, 451,327 CrazyGames plays paid 556.92 euros in 2018, net negative; ACRgames (2018) said $1,000 a month needs about 500K plays a month; a dev reports 1.20 euro per 1,000 plays. At that rate $1,000 needs about 800K plays a month. A guide says 80% of itch paid games earn $0-50 a month, and "$200-$2,000" for well-performing portal games (unsourced). PolyTrack (Kodub, Aug 2024) and codergautam (Nov 2024) say CrazyGames pays but give no figure.

3. **Channel.** CrazyGames accepts solo makers anywhere and gives new games an initial boost; payout minimum 100 euros. Poki hand-reviews and finalised 227 games in 2025. The boost size is not published.

4. **Rivals.** Thousands of games per portal; share needed is about 800K plays a month, unknown percent of any genre.

5. **Cost.** Near zero per player; hosting only.

6. **Scale.** More games, more portals (Poki, GameDistribution, Playgama). Stops at the QA gate and the 1 euro per 1,000 plays rate.

7. **Odds.** Working game in two weeks: 70%. $1,000 a month in six months: under 1 in 6. $10,000 a month in eighteen months: 3%. Shut out: 15%.
