## B. Dispute Reply ($9 flat): WEAKENED, close to killed
1. Leader: PASS. Chargeflow 25% of recovered, no minimum, 20,000+ merchants (https://www.chargeflow.io/pricing).
2. Left out: FAIL. Chargeflow has no minimum; nobody dated and small was found complaining.
3. Cannot copy: FAIL. Stripe's own Smart Disputes already builds and submits evidence, no setup, fee only on a win, and waives the $15 counter fee (https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/smart-disputes, https://support.stripe.com/questions/june-2025-pricing-updates-for-disputes). Chargeflow itself has $0 minimums and a startup discount of up to 70%. PayPal has nothing automatic (https://www.paypal.com/chargeback); that is the one open slice. Shopify built-in not confirmed.
4. Ten named buyers: FAIL. None found from any page.
5. Sum: FAIL. No sourced count of small merchants. Only datum: US merchants win about 54% of contested disputes, contest about half (https://chargeback.io/blog/how-often-do-merchants-win-chargeback-disputes). $1M at $9 = 111,000 disputes.
6. Payback: FAIL. Nobody to reach at the moment of a dispute.

Bottom already taken: ChargePay $19.99-99.99/month flat (https://apps.shopify.com/chargepay); WinDispute.pro about $9/month, Stripe, launched Aug 2025 (https://www.toolmage.com/en/tool/windisputepro/); Chargemate free for 3 cases a month, launched Apr 2026 (Indie Hackers); Paidback 15% capped $250; Disputifier; free templates everywhere.
Verdict: weakened. One-line fix: PayPal-only sellers, who get no automation. Still no named buyers, so I would not tell a friend to build it.
Odds: 100 paying in 90 days 1 in 15; $1M in three years under 1 in 100.
