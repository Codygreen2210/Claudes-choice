# Report A (PAYING bar, high ticket): AWS bill review page

**Bar chosen: PAYING.**

1. **What it is:** Upload your AWS Cost Explorer CSV, get a ranked list of savings in dollars, each tied to a line on your own bill so you can check it.
2. **Does it already exist?** Yes. Usage.ai's free calculator takes a Cost Explorer CSV or invoice, needs no sign-up, and emails a per-service savings breakdown (securityboulevard.com/2026/06/how-to-use-the-usage-ai-aws-savings-calculator). Also Harness savings assessment and digitalcoding.com AWS waste tool (seen in search results, not opened).
3. **Who pays:** Upwork catalog audit tiers $150 / $600 / $1,500; Fiverr "Quick Scan" from sanitised exports is $25. The $600 job is real, but the same upload-an-export job already sells at $25 and free.
4. **Proof number:** 3 dated asks from real people. Found 0.
5. **Odds:** working version in two weeks 5 in 6 / 100 free users in 30 days 2 in 6 / 100 paying in 90 days 1 in 20 / $1M a year in three years under 3% / someone bigger builds it in six months: already built.

**Arithmetic:** at $300 per audit, $1M needs about 3,300 audits a year; at $500, 2,000. Buyer pool estimate: 800k to 900k small AWS accounts (third-party estimate, countly.net, no AWS figure). The pool is large; the price is not holding.
