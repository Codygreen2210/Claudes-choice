# Checker report, round 23

## D. EPREL phone lookup: KILLED (as a free-with-data product)
Not a "stands". The data is real, but I found no money, no spread route and no gap.

**Records:** all 3 hold. I opened the fiche PDFs for A3090, A3526 and A3634 (eprel.ec.europa.eu/fiches/smartphonestablets20231669/Fiche_2247679_EN.pdf, Fiche_2402618_EN.pdf, Fiche_2556297_EN.pdf). They opened with no key and match the file (5 years, 1,000 cycles, repair C, classes as logged). All three are Apple, so this proves nothing about other makers.

**Exists already?**
- EPREL's own public site is a free consumer search (inventor's fetch showed only the script shell; the EC page says consumers can search and download sheets).
- Retailers and carriers host the sheets: Vodafone, Elkjop, Orange (search results).
- iFixit and Right to Repair Europe read 2,334 EPREL phone records in Sept 2026 and published findings, not a tool (https://repair.eu/?p=9668). That shows the data can be bulk read, but they may have had access I could not see.
- Skyynet, gsmarena, endoflife.date: I saw no per-phone label page on any of them. Skyynet's claim is unconfirmed. Not proof of a gap.

**Open access:** not established. I could not open EPREL's API or search endpoint; the inventor's was blocked and rate limited, and the "needs an e-seal" claim is secondhand. Single fiche PDFs are open, but you must know each ID.

**Change history:** no evidence found that EPREL exposes history.

**Buyer:** none found. No priced dataset or paying buyer surfaced.

**Spread:** no audience. Press covered the label as news, not a tool.

**Odds:** 100 free users in 30 days: 1 in 6. 100 paying customers in 90 days: under 1 in 50. $1M a year within three years: under 1 in 200.

## B. Apify Store ceiling
- **(a) Payout:** apify.com/partners/actor-developers says $1.6M paid monthly and 4,500 "community developers actively publishing". It gives no count of paid developers. The inventor's $356 average assumed all 4,500 are paid, so it is a floor on the typical earner, not a fact; the spread is surely skewed.
- **(b) Top named independent figure:** over $2,000 a month, Guillaume Lancrenon, post dated 2 Oct 2024 (https://blog.apify.com/turning-code-into-cash-with-apify/). Confirmed. Nothing higher found.
- **(c) Review:** the Store publishing terms describe no pre-listing review. Apify may unpublish or delete any Actor without notice, and removes ones broken over 30 days (https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions). Refusal at entry: no; removal after: yes. Inventor's "unknown" is now answered.
- Spot-checks: 2 of 2 hold (partners page, blog post).

**Marketplace route:** not worth another round. The only verified solo figure is about $2,000 a month, listings carry removal risk, and discovery is Store search only.

Searches or fetches used: 18 of 18.
