# Report B: marketplace that brings the buyers

**Bar chosen: PAYING.**

1. **What it is:** a pay-per-use data Actor on the Apify Store for one tech-sector source. No specific missing listing was verified (see 2).
2. **Does it already exist?** The obvious tech sources are taken: Product Hunt, Chrome Web Store, VS Code and Shopify App Store each have Apify actors (https://apify.com/automation-lab/producthunt-scraper, https://apify.com/getdataforme/vscode-extension-reviews-scraper/api, https://apify.com/ethanteague/shopify-app-store-scraper, https://apify.com/scraper-engine/chrome-web-store-scraper).
3. **Who pays:** Apify Store buyers who run an Actor in one click. New sellers can list at once; the review process was not found, so refusal rate and delay are unknown.
4. **Proof number:** paid runs in the first 30 days. A live cheap listing (Product Hunt, $0.005 per product) shows 2 total users.
5. **Odds:** working version in two weeks 5 in 6 / 100 free users in 30 days 1 in 6 / 100 paying customers in 90 days 1 in 20 / $1M a year within three years under 1 in 100 / someone bigger builds it within six months 2 in 6.

**Ceiling arithmetic:** $1.6M a month is about $19.2M a year over 4,500 developers, about $4,300 a year each on average. $1M a year is about 5% of the whole pool, to one person. Best named independent figure found: over $2,000 a month (Oct 2024). Nobody independent near $1M was found. Real ceiling for a solo seller: low thousands a month, a handful maybe higher.
**Fees and terms:** 20% commission, monthly payout. Store publishing terms not read; scraped-data legality is the seller's risk. Standard pricing sunsets October 2026, so pay-per-event only.
