## 1. The pitch

**One sentence:** Planner Press builds a tap-to-jump planner PDF for your tablet note app in about a minute, and it's free to make.

Alternates:
1. Pick the year, the pages and a color, and get a planner PDF with tabs that jump to every month, week and day.
2. A planner for your tablet where every tab and date is a tap, made for your year, free to make.
3. I built a small page that makes your 2027 planner PDF, linked tabs and all. Free to make, $9 once if you want the credit line gone.

## 2. Who it's for, and where they already look

These are guesses from what the product does, not from customer data. We have no customers yet.

1. **Students on a tablet.** Take notes in a note app and want a planner in the same app, with their own week start (Sunday or Monday) and only the pages they use. Likely look: Pinterest (searching "digital planner", "goodnotes planner"), TikTok and YouTube Shorts study videos, Etsy.
2. **Small business owners, tradespeople and freelancers who plan on a tablet.** Want a clean year, month, week and day layout they can write on with a pen, without paying for a bundle of pages they will not use. Likely look: Etsy, Pinterest, X and Bluesky (people who build in public), YouTube.
3. **Parents and anyone running a household calendar.** Want a big month page with room in each day box, a week page, and a notes section; portrait or landscape to match how they hold the tablet. Likely look: Pinterest, Instagram, Etsy.

## 8. What to measure after two weeks

Count, from the link's visit numbers and the Stripe dashboard:
1. Visits to the planner page, split by where they came from if possible.
2. Downloads of the free PDF.
3. $9 purchases.
4. Per platform: which posts sent the most visits.
5. Messages or comments from anyone who opened a PDF on a real tablet, and what they said.

These cutoffs are rough guesses for accounts this small, not benchmarks:

- **Keep going:** 150 or more visits and 15 or more downloads, or 1 or more $9 purchase, or 3 or more people telling us the links work (or exactly how they don't) on their tablet. Then do another 14 days and put more into the best platform.
- **In between:** 40 to 149 visits, or visits but no downloads. Look at the page and the video before posting more; people are coming but not finishing.
- **Stop:** under 40 visits and no downloads and no purchases after the 14 days. Posting more on the same plan will not change that.

## 9. What we don't know yet

- Nobody has opened a Planner Press PDF in GoodNotes, Notability, Xodo or any tablet app. The links are standard PDF links; whether each app follows them is unproven. Do this before spending effort on the plan, and if it fails, change the copy.
- There is no live link yet, and no way to count visits is set up. Section 8 needs that first.
- Image-to-caption matching: pin text in section 5 was matched to the image files from the text in `make-images.mjs`, not by looking at the pictures. Check each before posting.
- Etsy: rules for this kind of listing are unchecked. The $9 pass is set up through Stripe and lasts 31 days, and no way to hand an Etsy buyer a pass has been built. The listing is a draft only.
- The buyer groups in section 2 are guesses. No one has bought or asked for this yet.
- Platform limits (Pinterest title 100 and description 800 characters, YouTube title 100, Etsy title 140, tags 13 of 20) come from what I knew plus search results that did not show the numbers. Check them in each app on the day.
- Whether "$9 once" is still accurate: the file says the pass is good for 31 days of unlimited downloads. The copy says "once" to match the product page.
- Whether the free planner at 441 pages (everything on) is too large for some tablet apps is unknown.
