Live: https://lane-luck.vercel.app
Clips: `lane-luck-vertical.mp4` (1080x1920, 21 s) and `lane-luck-square.mp4` (1080x1080, 21 s). Share image: `../og.png`.

## What to expect (from round 28's checker, not a promise)
About 300 people in the first 30 days is the likely number. 100 if the posts sink, up to 8,000 if one catches. Roughly 1 in 8 to pass 1,000. One post is a lottery ticket, so the plan is several tickets in different places.

## Week 1: free posts

Post one place a day so you can tell which one moved. Reply to every comment the same day.

### Day 1: Hacker News (Show HN)
Rules: the maker posts it, the title starts with "Show HN:", no asking for upvotes. Weekday mornings US Eastern are the usual advice; that is hearsay, not something we measured.

Title:
> Show HN: Lane Luck – is the other checkout line really faster, or is it you?

URL: https://lane-luck.vercel.app

First comment (post it yourself right after):
> I build stuff on the side of a plant job. Everybody says they always pick the slow line, so I made a ten-round toy to check.
>
> Five lanes. You see how many people, how full the carts are and how quick the cashier is. You pick, all five run, and at the end it splits the time you lost into two piles: lost to luck (a price check, a declined card) and lost to your own pick (a lane that was shorter on paper was sitting right there).
>
> The snags are rolled before you pick, so your choice doesn't change them. Someone who always takes the best lane on paper wins about 7 of 10; picking blind wins 2 of 10. "How it works" at the bottom has the numbers.
>
> No sign-up, no cookies, one static page. Same link gives a friend the same ten rounds. Tell me where the model is wrong.

## Week 2 (maybe): $5 a day

Only if week 1 shows people finish the ten rounds and send the link on. If nobody shares for free, paid clicks will not either.

- **Budget:** $5 a day for 7 days, $35 total, hard cap. One platform.
- **Where:** Meta (Facebook and Instagram feeds and Reels) with the vertical clip. Check the minimum daily budget when you set it up; I did not verify today's minimums.
- **Who:** United States, adults, broad. Let the platform find them. No interest targeting to start.
- **Goal setting:** link clicks or landing page views, not "engagement".
- **Ad text A:** "You always pick the slow line. Or do you? Ten rounds, two minutes, no sign-up."
- **Ad text B:** "Is the other checkout line really faster? Find out if it's bad luck or you."
- **Stop rule, written now:** after 3 days ($15), if it has cost more than 50 cents a click, stop. After 7 days, keep going only if under 25 cents a click AND you can see people sharing.

## One thing to decide first
The page says "No tracking" and has none, so right now there is no way to count users, only clicks on the ad side. To count, turn on Vercel's visitor counts (no cookies) and change the footer line to "No cookies, no sign-up". Not done; your call.
