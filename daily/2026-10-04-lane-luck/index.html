A ten-round toy about checkout lines. No tracking, no sign-up.

How it works

Each cashier scans at a fixed pace, somewhere from 2.0 to 4.5 seconds per item. You see that as quick (under 2.8), steady (under 3.7) or slow. Every shopper also takes 25 seconds to pay.

Each shopper already waiting has a 12% chance of a hidden snag (price check, coupons, card declined, forgot an item, chatty) that adds 40 to 120 seconds. Snags are fixed by the seed in the link before you pick, so your pick never changes them.

For every lane we work out an "on paper" time: items times pace, plus payment, plus the average snag cost (9.6 seconds per waiting shopper), plus your own cart. A good pick is any lane within 5% of the best on-paper time.

Time lost each round is your lane's real time minus the fastest lane's real time. The part that is "your pick" is how much worse your lane was on paper than the best one. Whatever is left is luck. Picking at random wins 2 rounds in 10 on average.

A ghost copy of your cart joins every other lane, so all five are timed with the same cart.
