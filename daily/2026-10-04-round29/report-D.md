# Report D: phone-browser and short-video games

**Trend (opened sources):**
- Block Blast, a drag-and-clear puzzle with no leaderboard, had 368M downloads in 2025, D7 retention 13.4% ([maf.ad](https://maf.ad/en/blog/block-blast/)); its TikTok ads reuse player clips. Players chase their own score.
- Browser portals are big but anonymous: CrazyGames 250M plays, 30M monthly users, 2-5 minute sessions ([Conduit](https://conduitgg.substack.com/p/gen-alphas-favorite-game-store-is)). No phone share found.
- YouTube Playables: Tall Man Run 106M plays, 50+ markets ([Mobile Gamer](https://mobilegamer.biz/youtube-has-quietly-rolled-out-playables-across-europe/)). Discord Activities: Death by AI nearly 7M players ([Discord](https://discord.com/blog/build-where-the-world-plays)).
- Spread by clip: 67 Speed, a browser tap-race, claims 12k to 340k daily players in six days, Jan 2026, 61% of traffic from TikTok ([maker's blog, unverified](https://67speedgames.com/blog/67-tiktok-takeover/)). The film-worthy part was the player's face.
- Daily puzzles are shrinking: Wordle 4.2B plays in 2025 vs 5.3B in 2024 ([A Media Operator](https://www.amediaoperator.com/analysis/wordle-tv-show-new-york-times-games-jonathan-knight/)). No LinkedIn or Facebook Instant 2025 numbers found.
- Open to unknown makers with no gate: own site, CrazyGames (takes anyone). Gated: Poki (hand-picked), Discord (verification for discovery), YouTube Playables (no open path found).

## Idea 1: Ghost Wire
1. Drag a ball along a thin wire that shakes more the longer you go. Thumb drags.
2. Wire shows at once, thumb touches the start, no menu. First wobble costs a life; you reach 12 seconds, die near the end, think "one more go".
3. Skill is steadier hands and reading the bends; tension every second; beat your best distance.
4. Tomorrow: daily wire shape. Next week: a streak and your friends' ghost times.
5. Hot-wire games exist on portals. Twist: a link carries your recorded run as a ghost thread your friend races. Borrows Block Blast's beat-your-own-score loop and 67 Speed's face-on-camera tension.
6. Yes. Hard: shake feel and wire generation from a seed. No hand-made levels.
7. Post filmed runs with face in frame, put on CrazyGames. Players: low 50, likely 300, high 5,000.

## Idea 2: Half a Hand
1. Two people open one link; one drags the ball left-right, the other up-down, through a falling course.
2. Phone A makes a room link, friend taps it, ball drops at once. Near-miss laughing is the "again" moment.
3. Skill is calling out moves in time; goal is a deeper depth than your pair's last best.
4. Tomorrow: daily course, pair streak. Next week: harder seeds.
5. Borrows Death by AI (Discord group play, nearly 7M players) without needing Discord. Twist: split controls.
6. Yes. Hard: live sync of two thumbs (Supabase realtime). Courses from a seed.
7. Clips of two friends yelling; sent by link. Low 30, likely 150, high 3,000.
