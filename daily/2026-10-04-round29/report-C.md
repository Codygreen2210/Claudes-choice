# Scout C: phone games from one person or a tiny team

**The trend (five lines)**
1. Simple one-thumb loops with instant retry get the volume: Vampire Survivors mobile 5M downloads in about 3 months, peak 204k in a day ([source](https://mobilegamer.biz/vampire-survivors-mobile-hits-5m-downloads/)). It copied Magic Survival, so cloning a proven loop is normal.
2. Strangers arrive through one outside push: streamers lifted Suika from 2021 to 11M downloads ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suika_Game)); one TikTok (9M views) gave Bacon 18.5M ([source](https://mobilegamer.biz/bacon-the-game-hits-18-5m-downloads-after-going-viral-on-tiktok/)); Apple featured Psych 595 times ([source](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/addictive-indie-mobile-game-psych-scores-1m-downloads-in-two-weeks)).
3. Web tech works: a two-person team had 8 Poki games over 10M plays, about 800k plays a day, three months each ([Poki](https://poki.com/blog/how-emolingo-games-built-business-html5-web-games-poki)).
4. Solo Balatro mobile made about $937k in a week, but after a PC hit ([source](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-approaches-1-million-in-seven-days-on-mobile)): a head start.
5. A solo game with no loop to master made $3 ([itch devlog](https://itch.io/devlog/959144/my-first-finished-game-not-a-hit-but-it-hit-me-hard.amp)). Q4 2025 chart: solitaire 3.1M at #1 ([Sensor Tower](https://develop.sensortower.com/blog/top-5-free-games-q4-2025)).

Common thread: a skill you can feel improving, runs under two minutes, and something clip-worthy to watch.

## Idea 1: Slosh
1. Drag to tilt and release items into a jar of liquid; same-size items merge, and a wave rolls every 20 seconds that you must steer around. Thumb: drag and release.
2. First 30 seconds: three drops teach merging; the first wave nearly spills the jar and you save it by dropping a heavy piece. "One more go" is the near-spill.
3. Reason to play: aiming, planning merges, reading wave timing. Beat your last height; every drop matters.
4. Tomorrow: a daily seed, same drops for everyone, with a share link showing your height versus a friend's. Next week: new jar shapes unlocked by score.
5. Borrows from Suika (4M downloads in 5 weeks, 11M by Oct 2024). Different: the wave forces timing and the seed allows fair comparison. Own shapes and names.
6. Build: matter.js physics, shapes drawn in code, about 10 days. Hard part is balance. No levels: endless.
7. Finding: short screen-recorded clips of near-spills, itch.io and CrazyGames (takes anyone). 30 days: low 50, likely 500, high 20,000. Honest: clip luck decides.

## Idea 2: Orbit Ghost
1. Tap to hop a dot between rotating rings, dodging gaps. Thumb: one tap.
2. First 30 seconds: ring one is easy; ring four speeds up and a translucent ghost of the day's best run shows the route. "One more go" is dying one ring behind the ghost.
3. Reason to play: timing, pattern memory, beating a visible ghost.
4. Tomorrow: new daily ring layout and ghost. Next week: weekly best-run ghost.
5. Borrows from Psych (1M App Store downloads, six months) and Bacon-style retry. Different: the ghost and daily seed. Own art.
6. Build: canvas, about 7 days; levels are generated from a seed. Hard part is difficulty curve.
7. Finding: Poki-style portals, short clips. 30 days: low 30, likely 300, high 10,000.
