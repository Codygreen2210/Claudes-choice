## Top three
**1. Cannon Collapse.** Play: aim, release, watch a tower fall; one block left teeters. Come back: star counts on levels and a daily tower. Crowded: very. Smash Fest copies (Smash Fair, Smash Party) are already on stores, and Tower Crush 3D has sat on CrazyGames since 2019. An unknown maker stands out only by sound, haptics and a clean first shot. Riskiest: physics tuning that makes towers fall in satisfying, not random, ways. Players in 30 days: low 50, likely 300, high 3,000.

**2. Bolt Rush.** Play: plan order so the tray never jams; 3-minute score. Come back: weak; a seeded run is not enough, so give it unlocks tied to best score. Crowded: very (Screw Out on CrazyGames, many apps), but none as a timed run. Riskiest: generator and solver that make boards that feel fair. Likely 200 (low 30, high 2,000).

**3. Slosh.** Play: drop, merge, survive the wave. Come back: shapes and best height. Crowded: Suika clones everywhere. Riskiest: stable matter.js merge physics with liquid look. Likely 200 (low 30, high 5,000).

## Build check
Two weeks is enough for each with matter.js or plain canvas. Skipped sound and touch feel make all three feel cheap. Cannon Collapse needs per-material sound, screen shake and haptic buzz most.

## Pick: Cannon Collapse
It has the strongest proof (a 2026 #1 with no ads), skill you can feel, and instant retry. Be honest: the daily tower and ghost shots are extras, not the reason; the shot is. Test in a day: boxes for art, one tower, three shots; five people play without instruction. Watch whether anyone says "again" unprompted after a near miss, and whether they hit the replay before the result screen finishes. If fewer than three of five replay, drop it.
