# Scorecard (every idea is reported in this shape)

Each line needs a link or a plain "no evidence found". Odds are X in 6.

1. **What it is**, in one sentence a non-engineer follows.
2. **Does it already exist?** Closest three things, with links, and what is different in substance.
3. **Who pays or uses it, and where they are today** (a place a new account can post or list, with a link).
4. **The one number that would prove it**, and the cheapest test that produces that number (time, cost).
5. **Odds**, each stated separately: working version in two weeks / 100 FREE users in 30 days / 100 PAYING customers in 30 days / someone bigger builds it within six months.

The checker fills in a sixth line: **Checker's verdict** (stands, weakened, or killed) and why, with links.
