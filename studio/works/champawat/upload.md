DESCRIPTION
May 12, 1907, a river gorge in Kumaon, northern India. Almost 300 villagers beat drums along a ridge to drive one tigress toward a single man with a rifle: Jim Corbett. By most estimates she had killed 436 people in Nepal and India, the most by any single tiger on record.

This is the story of the Champawat tiger: how the Nepalese army drove her across the border, how she hunted, why she turned to people at all (the answer was in her teeth), the night in the ravine at Phungar, the beat in the Champa gorge, and how the man who shot her went on to help save India's tigers.
