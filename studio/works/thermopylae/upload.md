DESCRIPTION
480 BC. Xerxes of Persia marches the biggest army the world had ever seen into Greece. Waiting for him at a narrow pass called Thermopylae, the hot gates, are about 7,000 Greeks, and King Leonidas of Sparta with his 300.

For two days the Persians cannot get through. Then a local man named Ephialtes sells the king a secret: a goat path over the mountain. This is the story of the three days at the hot gates: the wall, the Immortals, the arrows that would block out the sun, the choice Leonidas made on the last morning, and what the little hill at Thermopylae still holds today.
