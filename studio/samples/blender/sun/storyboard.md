# "What if the Sun blinked out?" — storyboard (clay stop-motion, 9:16, poses on twos)

Sets: **A** tabletop world (grass slab, paper sky, clay Sun + Moon, lake and pond, trees, Ball);
**B** black-felt space table (chalk orbit, pushpin, string, clay Earth, Pluto, pinhead stars);
**C** cutaway tank (ice lid, swirling clay ocean, sea floor, vent, microbes).
Every cut is led 2 poses (1/6 s) before the line starts. Caption/label safe zones (1080x1920): hook word and labels live
in the top ~270 px, number readouts in a box ~250–410 px, captions ~1290–1420 px, subscribe button ~1590–1700 px in the
last 2.8 s. All key action is framed in the band ~430–1250 px; the sky/felt sits above it, the table below it.
No text anywhere in the 3D scene (the stopwatch, thermometer and calendar are blank clay; the burn-in adds numbers).
