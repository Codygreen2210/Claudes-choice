Royalty Free Sound Effects Archive: GameAudioGDC

Each year we give away thousands of dollars worth of sound effects for free in celebration of the Game Developers Conference. This is our archive. All of the sounds are royalty free and commercially usable. No attribution is required and you can use them on an unlimited number of projects for the rest of your lifetime.

All of the files we send out are just a small sample of our suppliers complete collection. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use these sound effects in commercial projects?

Yes! These sound effects are completely royalty-free for any commercial work. No fees, no paperwork. Use them in games you sell, YouTube videos you monetize, or client projects you invoice for.

Do I need to credit Sonniss when using these sound effects?

Nope. Attribution is always appreciated, but never required. Your project credits are yours to manage however you want.

How many projects can I use these free sound effects on?

Unlimited. Forever. One download covers you for life. These sound effects are yours to use across every project you ever work on.

Can I edit and modify the sound effects?

Absolutely. Layer them, stretch them, pitch them, mangle them - make them completely yours. Just don't claim you recorded the originals (we both know you didn't haul a Neumann into a volcano).

Can I resell or redistribute these sound effects?

Not as sound effects. That covers the original files and any edited or re-designed versions, on their own or inside a sound library, sample pack, asset pack or anything similar. But you can absolutely sell them as part of your finished game, film, app, or creative project. The license has the full details.

Can I use these sound effects for AI or machine learning training?

No. AI/ML training is strictly prohibited under our license. These professional sound effects are licensed exclusively for media production - games, films, podcasts, and creative projects.

What types of projects can I use these royalty-free sound effects in?

Games, films, TV, podcasts, YouTube, TikTok, VR/AR experiences, mobile apps, advertisements, trailers - basically any audio-visual project where sound effects make things better (which is all of them).

Do these sound effects ever expire?

Never. Your license lasts a lifetime. Use these sound effects on projects you're working on now, or projects you haven't even dreamed up yet. Ten years from now? Still covered.
