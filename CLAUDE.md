# Claudes-choice: rules for Claude

- **Music rule (from Cody):** anything that uses music must be run through the studio's ears before it's sent: `python3 studio/senses/listen.py file.wav --out notes/`. Fix what it flags (clipping, true peak over -1 dBTP, mud, harshness, buried or missing parts, wrong key/tempo) and look at the picture, not just the numbers. This covers build time-lapse music, LaunchReel tracks, promos, and any generated audio.
  Needs: `pip install --break-system-packages soundfile scipy librosa pyloudnorm matplotlib`.
- Daily builds live in `daily/<date>-<name>/` on their own branch; Cody merges. No "ship" language.
- **Story films (from Cody, Oct 12 2026, replaces the Oct 10 silhouette rule):** the flat silhouette look is retired; Cody called the Champawat Short "anything but the quality I've been asking for" (kneel looked like the body snapping in half, rifle not aimed at the tiger, grey on grey so nothing read on a phone). New look: Style B from `studio/works/style-tests/` (Cody picked it Oct 12): painted storybook look, realistic proportions, faces and clothes (our own original designs), haze, light rays, depth-of-field blur on the foreground, grain, and depth in every shot (foreground / midground / background layers, parallax camera, light and shadow, strong figure-ground contrast). Every pose that bends (kneel, aim, run) is checked on the model sheet in motion, and every shot is checked as a phone-size frame sheet before anything is sent. Voice: the studio voice in `film_voice.py` (style B, less rasp), numbers written as spoken words with no hyphens.
- **Shapes and motion:** every new figure goes on a model sheet (`model.html`) and is looked at alone before it goes into a scene. Anything that walks or runs: draw eight phases in order and check a lifted foot travels toward the nose.
- **Sound (from Cody, Oct 11 2026):** sound effects come from the studio's library, `studio/sfx/foley.py` (catalog in `studio/sfx/CATALOG.md`): recorded CC0 samples in `studio/sfx/samples/` layered with synth parts (sub weight, long tails, variation). Only add recordings whose author or pack states CC0, and log each one in `studio/sfx/samples/SOURCES.md`; no Pixabay, CC BY-NC or unclear licences. GitHub is the only sample source this workspace can reach; for anything else, ask Cody to download it. When a story needs a sound the library doesn't have, add it to the library (not inline in the film's score), run `python3 studio/sfx/audition.py <name>`, fix what it flags (including "phone speakers will barely hear it") and look at its tile on the sheet before using it. The library grows with every film.
- **Music pacing (from Cody, Oct 11 2026):** the music must follow the story, not sit on one bed all the way through. Score every film scene by scene with `studio/instruments/moods.py` (each scene gets a mood and an energy; moods set their own tempo, mode and band), then run `python3 studio/senses/pacing.py music.wav plan.json --out notes/` alongside listen.py and fix what it flags. Hand-written moments (a hit on a cue word, a motif) go on top of the mood beds.
- **YouTube (from Cody, Oct 11 2026; full research in `docs/youtube-growth-2026.md`):** the channel is History_Unfoldz, posted through Metricool. Apply to every film and Short:
  - **Topic:** pick by search demand (Studio > Analytics > Trends, autocomplete). Every film must be clearly its own story with its own sources; never a template with names swapped (YouTube's inauthentic-content rule is channel-wide).
  - **Title:** the hook plus the searchable name of the event ("The 5 Minutes That Won the Pacific War | The Battle of Midway"). Honest; no "secret they hid" bait.
  - **Thumbnail:** 2 to 4 words that add to the title, high contrast at phone size (dark ground behind cream text, red on the one key shape). Check it at 320 px wide.
  - **Opening:** start mid-story on the title's promise. No logo, date card or slow fade first. Aim for 60%+ still watching at 0:30.
  - **Length:** 8 minutes is the floor; try 10 to 14 on topics that hold people.
  - **Description:** names, year and event in the first lines, chapters from `timing.json` (first at 0:00, at least 3), short sources list, few tags (misspellings only).
  - **Shorts swipe test (from Cody's first Short, Oct 12 2026: 85% swiped away, stalled at 667 views):** the picture fills the whole 9:16 frame (reframe each scene for vertical, no letterboxed strip with dead space), text sits small on the picture, motion and voice start at 0.0 s with no fade from black, the first line is the most dramatic one, and the last shot cuts cleanly back into the first so it loops. Check "Viewed vs. swiped away" in Studio; aim for 60%+ viewed.
  - **Shorts first second (from Cody's Studio numbers, Oct 12 2026: 83-91% swiped away even full-frame):** open on the scene's biggest moment (`--flash`), say a hook a stranger understands at 0.0 s and show it big (`--hook-say/--hook-show`: "3 carriers. 5 minutes.", "$250,000 a day. Then this."), picture already moving (push-in), then tell how it happened, trimmed to its best 25-30 s (`--start/--end`). Never open on a still frame, a date, or a line that needs context ("Patterson stayed."). Compare each Short's Viewed % against the 8-17% baseline.
  - **Shorts to films (from Cody, Oct 12 2026: Shorts got views but didn't convert):** the Related video link is the only tappable path from a Short to its film (links in Short descriptions and comments aren't clickable). Every Short ends by pointing at it: said ("the full story is linked, right above the title") and shown low on the last frame (`make_vertical_short.py` does both by default). Cut so the Short stops at the cliffhanger, not after the payoff. Remind Cody to set the Related video link on every Short.
  - **Shorts:** one complete scene from the film, cold open on action, its own ending, then one spoken question only the film answers (`studio/shorts/make_vertical_short.py`; the older `make_story_short.py` letterboxes, don't use it). 2 to 4 a week, never repost the same clip, each Short gets a different scene. Clean files only when cross-posting; off YouTube the end line must say where the film is.
  - **Settings:** not made for kids, every time. Education category. AI disclosure off for stylized animation unless something could pass as real footage.
  - **After Metricool posts it, Cody does a 2-minute desktop Studio pass** (the API can't): Related video link on each Short pointing to its film, end screen on each film, Test & Compare on thumbnails once a film has impressions. Remind him in the hand-off.
  - **Judge** over 10+ uploads: traffic sources, then CTR, then 30-second hold. Numbers mean little under 100 views. Never delete or re-upload a flop.
